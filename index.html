Mechatronics Lab

Wind Turbine

Converted "wind" provided from a fan into a usable DC voltage. This was done by converting the AC voltage from a generator into DC with a "Smoothed" Full Wave Rectifier. There was also a light system to indicate the amount of voltage being supplied: Red = Low, Yellow = Medium & Green = High. An Arduino was used to control the logic of this circuit, and a capacitor was added to the rectifier to minimise any ripple.
